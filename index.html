The weekend of the wedding

The weekend of the 25th-27th is La Mercè weekend, celebrating the Patron Saint of Barcelona with massive street festivities. There are multiple traditional Catalan events, activities & concerts that we would love to share with you.

You don’t really need to plan for them and will most likely run into them if you’re walking downtown, and we will coordinate which events we attend on Saturday afternoon/Sunday as the date nears.

Some opinions about Barcelona

Moving Around Barcelona:

The most convenient way to move around the city is the metro, or walking. The metro will run 24-7 through La Mercè weekend, including the whole 24-26 night.

Getting to / from the airport:

Aerobús is easy: A1 serves T1 and A2 serves T2, and both run 24/7 to Plaça Catalunya. FREENOW (app for local taxis) and recently Uber work in Barcelona, after a rather tense history with the taxi guild (feel free to go on a news rabbit hole).

Safety:

While Barcelona is extremely safe in terms of violent crime, pickpocketing is extremely common. Be extremely cautious, do not have valuables in an outer pocket in a backpack, do not leave anything unattended. A fanny pack is a solution. Yes this can happen to you: multiple people attending the wedding have already been pickpocketed in Barcelona, and yes, these people have been all over the world without being pickpocketed. Barcelona is currently SOTA in PickPocketBench.

Where to eat & drink (& please don’t eat in “Las Ramblas”)

1881 / La Terrassa de les Indianes: Go for a drink and the port view; that’s really why we like it. Plaça de Pau Vila, 3, Barceloneta.

La Cova Fumada: Tiny, old school and no fuss. Go for tapas and a bomba. Carrer del Baluard, 56, Barceloneta.

Pepa Tomate: Multiple locations, lunch special and traditional dishes (ask for “menu of the day” not just “menu”).

La Pallaresa: A classic for churros and very thick hot chocolate. Carrer de Petritxol, 11, Gòtic.

4 Gats: Historic Modernista place where Picasso used to hang around. We mostly like it for the room and the history.

7 Portes: Cool space and traditional food.

Can Paixano (El Xampanyet): Busy tapas bar, extremely crowded (it’s good and cheap) but very nice.

Bosc de les Fades: A completely ridiculous fairy forest bar next to the Wax Museum. Very touristy, but fun at least once. Passatge de la Banca, 7, Gòtic.

Bar Marsella: Old, scruffy and famous for absinthe. It feels like old Barcelona. Carrer de Sant Pau, 65, Raval.

Mercat de Santa Caterina: Grocery market with produce, tapas bars and prepared food to go.

Dr. Stravinsky, Bestiari, Bareto Olímpic, Boadas: A list of bars we like.

Compartir: On the fancy side, ~70 EUR/pax & needs reservation on weekends, it’s the tapas bar of a renowned many-Michelin-star chef.

La Central del Raval: Bookshop with a hidden courtyard café. Nice place to stop if you’re wandering around Raval. Carrer d’Elisabets, 6, Raval.

El Nacional: Beautiful food hall just off Passeig de Gràcia. Easy place to pop in for tapas or a drink. Passeig de Gràcia, 24 Bis, Eixample.

Some food we miss when we are abroad

Tapas of many sorts: There are many little nibbles to try, you’ll try most of them at the wedding! Truita de patates / tortilla de patatas (potato omelette). You’ll see it everywhere. Some are amazing; some are sad. Many types of cured meat (pernil/jamón) is the most famous one, fuet is the one we miss the most when abroad. Many types of seafood, such as chocos (fried cuttlefish), pop/pulpo (octopus), boquerones / seitons (anchovies) pickled in vinegar. They’re usually accompanied by pa amb tomàquet (tomato bread).

Paella & fideuà: Rice/pasta with seafood. For either one, we’d go somewhere that actually does rice and seafood rather than ordering one at random on La Rambla. Arròs negre, black from the cuttlefish ink, is our favorite variation.

Something sweet: Crema catalana, mel i mató, ensaïmada (the original version is not vegetarian, but most of them are nowadays, ask the bakery!), flan, and chocolate + churros. The chocolate should be thick enough to eat with a spoon.
